Pace Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Applies to the Pace Pulse apps for Android phones and Wear OS watches

In short: Pace Pulse collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your workouts stay on your device.

What the app stores

To show your history, streaks and badges, Pace Pulse saves the following only on your device:

  • Completed workouts: plan name, start and end time, minutes walked, rounds completed and your optional effort rating.
  • Custom workout plans you create.
  • Your settings, such as voice and sound choices, weekly goal and reminder time.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot transmit data.

What the app does not collect

No location, no steps, no heart rate or other health sensor data, no contacts, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, and no crash or usage analytics.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup, your workout history and settings are included in your own device backup (for example, your Google account backup) so you keep your streak on a new phone. That backup is managed by Android under your account, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: show the running timer with pause, skip and end controls, and the optional daily reminder.
  • Foreground service: keep the timer and cues running while the screen is off.
  • Vibration: haptic cues when a phase changes.
  • Wake lock: keep cues on time while a workout is running. It is released when the workout pauses or ends.

Voice guidance

Spoken cues use the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. Pace Pulse only selects voices that work offline.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Privacy → Delete all workout history, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything Pace Pulse stored.

Children

Pace Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
